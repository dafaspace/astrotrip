One place for every answer from the 10-20 astrologers asked to find a wrong fact (decided by Dafa
29 Oct 2026; `docs/promo-stage-1.md`). Each tester has full access for life, given with
`node tools-access-key.mjs issue life "Name"`.

For every report: the date, the tester, what they compared against (their program, the chart
data), what differed, and what was done about it. A difference is a finding until it is shown to be
the other program's error or a setting, and then it says so.

**The paying question**, asked once at the end of a week of use: "After a week with everything,
would you pay $9.99 once for transits, progressions, returns and synastry?" Read on 15 Nov 2026
and recorded as **stated intent**, a weak signal: a pre-order or a real payment outranks it.
Three or more "yes" and the paid version goes ahead (CLAUDE.md, Money, point 6).
